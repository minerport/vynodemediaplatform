# The VyNode experience

VyNode applies cinematic polish to clear media organization without copying
another product's trade dress. Its identity is quiet, warm, precise, and
media-led. Everyday language is about media, people, libraries, and watching;
servers and network details appear only when they help someone decide or recover.

## Principles

### Media leads

Artwork and content establish hierarchy. Navigation and application chrome remain
quiet, and controls recede when they are not needed.

### Progressive disclosure

Present the next useful action and the context needed to choose it. Technical media,
network, and identity details belong in secondary panels or Advanced diagnostics.

### Spatial consistency

Navigation, page identity, primary actions, metadata, status, and player controls
keep predictable locations within each platform's interaction model.

### Calm density

Use space efficiently without crowding. Media grids may expand; prose and forms use
readable maximum widths. Avoid decorative nested containers.

### Cinematic detail

Backdrops, posters, gradients, type, and motion make details immersive while
preserving contrast and unambiguous action priority.

### Platform native

Web uses pointer and keyboard conventions, phone uses thumb-reachable touch
navigation, and TV uses deterministic D-pad focus with couch-readable scale. Shared
identity does not require pixel-identical layouts.

### Accent identity

VyNode uses flat neutral black/charcoal chrome with a restrained warm-orange
semantic accent. Orange is limited to Play/Resume, selected state, progress,
short status cues, and the dedicated focus ring. The reference establishes the
flat visual character, but VyNode retains its own values, spacing, brand,
components, and interaction model rather than copying its trade dress.

Semantic success, warning, danger, and information colors remain independent from
the product accent. Orange communicates VyNode identity and primary interaction;
status colors communicate state only.

### Phase 15 Windows handoff

The Windows client must inherit the Phase 14.5 identity without reinterpretation:
flat black/charcoal surfaces, warm orange `#F4A340`, focus orange `#FFD08A`,
artwork-first Home and library browsing, controlled cinematic details, compact
navigation, global-account-first onboarding, simple multi-server switching, and
unmistakable Play/Resume priority. Windows must not revive mint or introduce a
new product accent.

## Television interaction model

TV uses a dedicated shell rather than scaled phone navigation. A compact rail is
always locatable at the safe left edge; it expands over a dimmed content layer when
focused, so content never jitters or reflows. Destination selection is a quiet tint
and label color, while current focus is the strong couch-visible ring.

Initial focus is deterministic: feature Play/Resume on Home, first artwork on media
libraries, query entry on Search, and Play/Resume on details. Left from the leftmost
eligible target enters the rail. Right returns to that destination's content entry.
Back closes player menus first, exits playback to its detail origin, returns details
to their originating Home/library/Search surface, and toggles between content and
rail at top level. Show episode identity is retained across player return.

### Honest status

Say whether Connect, a media server, or the device network is unavailable. Say when
a download remains playable. Never collapse these states into a generic failure.

### Few primary actions

Play or Resume normally dominates. Start Over, watchlist, favorite, download, and
overflow actions remain visibly secondary.

### Purposeful motion

Motion communicates focus, navigation, hierarchy, or state. It is restrained and
disabled when reduced motion is requested.

## Product model

The normal identity is a VyNode Account. One account discovers its authorized
servers and libraries without address entry. With one server, selection chrome stays
hidden. With several, a compact named selector changes scope; it does not imply
unsupported aggregation. Consumer status uses names and availability, never raw
addresses. Network details live under Advanced.

## Consumer, settings, and administration

Consumer surfaces are cinematic and artwork-forward. Administrative surfaces use
the same color, type, controls, and state language but favor compact tables, grouped
forms, and operational hierarchy. Settings use category navigation, constrained
forms, strong labels, and supporting explanations.

## Visual review

For each capture ask what is seen first; whether that is correct; whether space,
containers, and action emphasis are justified; whether artwork has enough presence;
whether a new user can predict the next action; and whether the result feels
deliberately finished on that platform.
